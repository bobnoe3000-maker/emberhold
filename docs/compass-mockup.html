Compass travel: mockup

A small compass sits under the minimap. Tap it for a short pick list of places that depend on where you are. Pick one and the party walks there on its own, using the tap-to-move path with its gold ring and trail. A chip above the party cards shows the destination and cancels it; the stick cancels it too. Every row shows how far away the place is and, in dungeons, the room's level coloured against your own.

In town

Just two places: the square (the services menu) and the road out. In the square itself the compass stays up beside the service bar (changed after play-testing: it's the quickest way back out of town).

Off-screen destination: the trail ends in an arrow at the screen edge. Arriving (or entering a hostile room) ends the walk; the stick or ✕ cancels it.

Walking

The party walks on its own along the trail. The chip, just above the party cards where your thumb is, names the destination and the steps left; tap ✕ or touch the stick to take over.

Behaviour

  • The list is built from the world when opened: undiscovered and discovered rooms, props, the exit, and outdoor sites and exits.
  • Steps are the path length from the same pathfinder as tap-to-move. Places you can't reach don't appear.
  • “Room at your level” picks the nearest room whose level is at or below yours.
  • “Nearest unexplored” overland means a site never entered, remembered in the save.

In battle

  • In a dungeon, a walk stops when the party enters a room with enemies, and the fight starts as usual. The chip changes to “⚔ Resume → Stairs down”; tap it to carry on through the room, since rooms respawn until you leave.
  • A walk picked in the middle of a fight doesn't stop for that room, so the compass can take you out of a bad fight.
  • Routes prefer corridors. A route passes through a room only when there's no other way.

Placement

  • A 44 px round button under the minimap on the right, in the same dark and gold style as the service bar. It stays up in a town square too (changed after play-testing).
  • The list opens beneath the button with one tap and closes on a pick, a tap outside or the stick.
