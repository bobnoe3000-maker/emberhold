What I need from you

  1. Direction: A (the Chronicle), B (Ember diorama) or C (Ash and flame)? Mixing is fine, for example B's scenes with A's manuscript text panel.
  2. Sound: the score that comes with your direction, or another one? Text only, or should the lines be read aloud later (that needs a recorded voice)?
  3. Length: about 70 seconds read at a normal pace, six cards, tap to advance, Skip always showing. Keep it, trim to four cards, or let cards advance on their own?
  4. The script: the six lines come from the world history and stop short of the Act IV secret. Anything you'd change?
  5. Splash and loading: the studio name on the splash is a placeholder ("Emberhold"). And should loading tips be lore fragments, wry one-liners, or both (as now)?
