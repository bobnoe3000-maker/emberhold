# The Greywater Fens' dungeons against the dungeon rules

**Status:** Analysis (2026-11-14), for the owner's decisions (§4). The raid cap (§3) is built.

The owner, 2026-11-14: *"Raid dungeon no more than 2 floors like our overall dungeon rules. Also check saltmere
dungeons do they follow our design rules?"*

The rules are the owner's for the Vale ([vale-dungeons-plan.md](./vale-dungeons-plan.md): *"no more than 5 rooms or large
corridors total per floor … hallways … at least 8 wide. No more than 2 levels down … Dressing and even layout should fit to
the dungeon type"*, decided as halls 10 wide, the same layout every visit) and [level-design-rules.md](./level-design-rules.md)
§1.1 (visibility), §4.1 (layout), §4.3 (dressing caps) and §4.4 (keep-clear). The Vale's plan ended *"The other lands when the
owner says the Vale is right"*: the Fens were never brought over. Saltmere's dungeons are the Fens': Toadking's Mound,
the Canal Locks, the Drowned Abbey, the Mere Tower and the secret Reedholm Undercroft.

no** (the Vale: 18 floors, 0). §4.3 caps: every room under its size's caps.

## 3. Built now: a raid runs two floors at most

`raid.js` `RAID.floors = 2`, `raidFloors(site)`: a raid runs the dungeon's last two floors (a two-floor one, both). It
sets out at the first one's stair, whose way up now leaves the raid (the compass reads *Leave the raid*); its last boss is
still the run's end and the coffer's. Party Up says *2 floors*; the raid card counts the raid's own floors. Tested
(`test/raid.test.mjs`).

Measured (`tools/balance/raid.mjs`, a fighter with Any + Any, six seeds, the band's top and middle), before → after:

| Dungeon | Won (top · middle) | Median run (top) | Gear a run found before its coffer (top) |

## 4. Proposed: the Fens authored as the Vale is, and the decisions

Bring the three band dungeons onto the Vale's rules, as plans (`plans.js`, `checkPlan`): two floors, five spaces a
floor, halls 10 wide, the same layout every visit, the band's three levels over the two floors (a floor's first rooms at
base + floor − 1, its offshoot and its hall a level up). The Tower and the Undercroft stay one floor; their halls go to 10.
The two visibility misses get fixed with it.

Decisions for the owner:

1. **Authored, as the Vale.** The same rooms in the same places every visit (the seed moves the furniture and the
   foes). *Recommended.* The other way: keep them generated, cut to two floors and five rooms, halls 10.
2. **The Abbey's three bosses on two floors.** *Recommended:* Teague in floor 1's offshoot (his cage, a dead end off the
   cloister), the Drowned Choir in floor 1's hall, the Abbess Below in floor 2's chapter-house. The other way: drop one
   of the three to a waystation's quest elsewhere.
3. **Where the story's floors go.** *Recommended:* the Mound's second and third floors become its first and second (Wren
   and the Toadking on floor 2, the burying-ground its hall); the Locks' Sluice on floor 1, Vat Seven its floor 2; the
   Abbey as in 2. Every quest's journal line and count rewritten to match, with a save migration for a quest under way.
4. **Order.** The Mound first (the Fens' way in, level 10), then the Locks, then the Abbey, each its own commit with its
   plan drawn (`tools/dungeon/vale-plans.mjs` grows a Fens mode), balance runs at the band's levels and captures.
