Gear and loot: the character sheet

Tap any party card to open that member's sheet: the gear they're wearing, their stats with the gear's share shown in green, and the party bag. Tap an item, worn or in the bag, to see its description, stats and a comparison with what's worn now. Icons are baked from the same KayKit models the figures carry (tools/actor-lab/icons.cjs), so a sword's icon is the sword in the hand. Slots and rarities follow GDD §8.
