The chat dock

A working mockup. The fight is a stand-in drawn on a canvas, but it runs the whole time: waves come and fall, health moves, and the HUD keeps counting behind the chat.

Try it

  • Swipe left on the party tiles to show the chat. Swipe right to get the tiles back. The Party · Chat dots above do the same with a tap.
  • Drag the handle at the top of the chat up to open it over most of the screen, down to close it again. A tap on the handle toggles it.
  • Opened wide, the chat shows the company's health in a strip at its top, so a fight going wrong is never out of sight.
  • ◐ sets how much of the fight shows through: clear, dim or solid.
  • World events arrive every few seconds in gold, with a button where there's something to do. With the chat out of sight they show as a short notice and a count on the Chat dot.
