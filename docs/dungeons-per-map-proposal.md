so players can replay them. Art, build props in code, old skarn stays as a guest boss. We can extend dungeons later to
cover up to 4 or 5 levels if needed and in the future we can have additional island maps to carry up to level 75 and
beyond."* So:

## 0. The rule: one dungeon a level

**Every level a player grinds at belongs to exactly one dungeon.** At level 5 there is one place to go: the dungeon
whose band holds 5. How many dungeons a map has follows from how many levels it spans, three to a dungeon, so there's
no cap per map to keep.

- **A dungeon is a band of three levels:**
  - three floors, a level each;
  - every room on a floor at its floor's level, but the two rooms before its hall a level up, capped at the band's
    top (rooms still pay for their danger); the hall keeps its floor's level;
  - its boss in floor 3's last hall, at the band's top.
- **The bands run on without a gap or an overlap:** 1–3, 4–6, 7–9, 10–12 and so on, across the maps.
- **What doesn't count, because it isn't a grind:**
  - **A secret dungeon**, one a map: a one-time delve, kept as you asked. The map's Chronicle opens it, it stands at
    the map's top level, and once its last hall is cleared it stays quiet: no waves come back. Its prize is its
    heirloom, its fragments and the story, not XP by the hour.
  - **The Mere Tower** pays no XP (v1.31), so it competes with no band. It stays a challenge.
- **No endless descent.** The Old Barrows going on down would overlap every band.

### What a dungeon's look is built from today

- **The layout:** caverns (dug) or halls (built), v1.46.
- **A theme** (`level.js THEMES`): a wall material, a floor mix and a hazard colour. Ten themes, several shared.
- **The room dressing** (`world.js`) is picked by **who holds the site**, not where it is:
  - the living get storehouse, camp or colonnade rooms;
  - the dead get colonnade, crypt or ossuary;
  - the chapel's and the drowned get nave, crypt or ossuary.
- **The props** are the same set everywhere: procedural voxel pieces drawn in code (`gsprite.js`: pillars, sarcophagi,
  pews, bones, crates, barrels, sacks, bedrolls, braziers, spires, monoliths, totems, gibbets, cages, shrines).
- **The art we have** for more: KayKit's medieval pack (barrels, crates, sacks, tents, a weapon rack, flags,
  scaffolding, rocks, a mine) and the nature pack (rocks, pebbles, mushrooms, dead trees). Nothing made for a crypt
  or a cave: no stalactites, urns, candles, niches or altars.
- **NPCs** can already stand in a dungeon: a *found* companion at the back of a floor (Brannoc, Wren) and a *visitor*
  in the entrance (the Kindler).

## 2. The plan

### 2.1 The rule

- **A map** is a land with its own overland: the Vale, the Fens, later the Reach and the rest.
- **At most three grinding dungeons a map, each one band of three levels.** The Vale is levels 1–3, 4–6 and 7–9.
- **Each dungeon is three floors, one level each.**
  - Floor 1 is the band's first level, floor 3 its last, and every room on a floor is that floor's level.
  - The boss holds floor 3's last hall, at the band's top.
  - A player who wants level-5 rooms goes to the second dungeon's second floor. Nothing is guesswork.
- **Each dungeon has its own:**
  - look: layout, floor, walls, light;
  - dressing kit for each floor;
  - foes;
  - an NPC at its entrance;
  - a boss, with one thing it does.
- **No endless descent.** The Mere Tower is the endless climb.

### 2.2 The Hollow Vale, levels 1–9: which three?

| Option | The three | What leaves the Vale | What it costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| A, the story's spine | Mill 1–3, Keep 4–6, Chapel 7–9 | the Barrows, the Warren | the Barrows are the Vale: the intro, the barrows road, the Standard, three fragments and five quests. Cutting them guts Act I |
| **B, recommended** | **Barrows 1–3, Keep 4–6, Chapel 7–9** | the Mill, the Warren | Act I's opener moves from the Mill to the Barrows; the Standard falls at 3, not 9 |
| C | Mill 1–3, Keep 4–6, Barrows 7–9 | the Chapel, the Warren | Act I's last chapter loses its place |

**B keeps the Vale's two threads:**
- the barrows road (the dead who keep their orders): levels 1–3;
- the Redhand's dig: the Keep (4–6) down to the Chapel (7–9), where the legion was bound and Act I ends at level 9.

### 2.6 The Fens to the same rule (a second step)

- **The Fens become levels 10–18:**

  - the Locks 13–15, its third floor the Sickpools' *Vat Seven* (the two chapters meet there);

### 2.7 How the look gets made

- **Dressing kits, per site and floor** (data in `sites.js`, `dress: { 1: 'dig', 2: 'gallery', 3: 'muster' }`),
  replacing the family-picked set. Each kit names:
  - its room themes;
  - its wall dressing;
  - its scatter;
  - its light's tint;
  - its floor and wall materials.
- **New props:**
  - from the packs we have, baked with `bake-env.cjs`: tents, weapon racks, flags, scaffolding, rocks, mushrooms;
  - procedural ones for what nothing has: stalagmites, urns, candle clusters, niches, an altar, chains, a font, spoil
    heaps.
  - Optionally, KayKit's *Dungeon Remastered* pack (CC0, the characters' maker), for crypt and keep furniture in
    the same hand.
- **Wall dressing** is new to the renderer: niches, banners and torches drawn against wall faces.
- **Pools:** shallow standing water painted on the floor, walkable (no impassable pools in rooms, v1.29).
- **Light:** each kit's tint on the ambient and on its braziers and candles.

## 3. The critique

Read back as the owner and as a player would.

1. **The Standard at level 3 spends the Vale's best moment in its first hour.** It's a set piece: the lamp, 240
   souls, the road opening and the carts running again. Today it falls at 9. At 3 it lands before the player has a
   party or knows the legion's story (the Chronicle is a slow read). It's the strongest objection to option B.
2. **The Vale loses its cave.** B's three are a crypt, a fort and a chapel: no pools, stalactites or boulders, though
   the owner named a cave as the example. The cave kit would be built for a Reach that isn't there.
3. **"Re-use in other maps" is really "shelve".** The Reach is milestones away. The Warren, Old Skarn, the drum and
   Hedda's goblins vanish from play until then; Hedda's rewrite swaps a light, funny quest for a third Redhand errand.
4. **One level a floor flattens the rooms.** Today a floor's rooms rise by their order from the entrance, so a party
   can step into a room one up for more pay (*waves that pay for their danger*, a key pillar). Flat floors lose that
   choice inside a dungeon.
5. **Nine levels a map doesn't reach 75.** The plan of record is six regions to 75 in bands of 15 (GDD §17). Three
   bands of three is nine a map: 75 would take eight or nine maps, or a lower cap, or wider bands later. The plan
   quietly assumes one of these.
6. **The Fens step rewrites Act II.** Six chapters, Wren's arc, the trials and the Tower's gate move by two or three
   levels, and the Locks–Sickpools merge changes two chapters' places. That's as big as the Vale step, and nobody
   has asked for it yet.
7. **Wall dressing is a renderer feature, not a prop.** Walls are height columns, and many are cut to knee-high stubs
   toward the camera (v1.46). A banner or a niche on a wall the camera can't see is wasted. Stalactites hang from a
   ceiling the cutaway doesn't show: in this view a cave reads by its floor (pools, stalagmites, rubble, boulders)
   and by its walls' broken line, not by what hangs.
8. **More props can clutter the arena.** Rooms are battle arenas: the middle stays open (GDD §3), and figures must
   read against the floor. Richer dressing has to stay at the walls and in the thirds, and stay dark enough.
9. **The KayKit dungeon pack is a new asset source.** It's CC0 and in the characters' style, but it's a new download
   in `fetch-assets.sh` and a credits line, and its colours would need the bake's grade to sit in our gloom.
10. **Saves.** A visit under way in the Mill or the Warren, quest counts keyed to them, the Ninth Milestone revealed,
    Brannoc's place, a felled Skarn: all need a migration (v31), and an old save must load or be refused cleanly.
11. **Three dungeons may be thin for nine levels.** Levels 1–9 are about 4½ hours of fighting at v1.38's pace, about
    1½ hours a dungeon. Each needs three floors that feel different, or the grind turns into one room seen 90 times.
12. **New NPCs are writing.** Three entrance NPCs need Ink, voice and canon (world doc first), and they're only worth
    it if they say something new.

## 4. The plan revised

The critique changes six things; the rest stands.

1. **The Vale's three stay B (Barrows 1–3, Keep 4–6, Chapel 7–9), but the road's climax moves to the end of the
   Vale.** Two ways to do it, for you to pick:
   - **4a (recommended):** the Barrows hold the road's first two ranks (Maudry's front rank, Osric's officers), and
     **the Standard holds the Chapel's Binding Crypt**. The legion was bound there, so its standard stands over the
     binding. The Robed Stranger works the dig on floor 2, and the Standard is the last hall at 9. Act I ends with
     the lamp breaking and the road opening: the Vale's story in one place.
     - The Barrows' own boss at 3 is new: **the Quartermaster**, a bright-eyed officer who rations the dead's weapons
       (a small mechanic: his rank re-arm on his call).
   - **4b:** keep the Standard at the Barrows' 3 and accept the early climax.
2. **The Vale keeps a cave: the Keep's third floor.** The Redhand dug through the cellars into a natural cave under
   the imperial stone: pools, stalagmites, boulders, a dripping fringe at the walls. It's the cave kit's first use,
   and the Keep's floors become a fort's, a barracks' and a cave's.
3. **Rooms still pay for their danger, inside the band.** A floor's level is the band's base + its number − 1, as
   planned, but **the two rooms before its hall are a level up, capped at the band's top** (as built: the hall keeps
   its floor's level, so a floor's boss meets the party at the floor's own level):
   - floor 1: 1–2;
   - floor 2: 2–3;
   - floor 3: 3 (the boss's floor).

   The band stays 1–3 and the choice comes back.
4. **Old Skarn stays in the Vale as a guest.** His band came down from the hills to sell the Redhand stolen hens:
   he's the Bailey's mid-floor boss (the Keep's first floor, level 4, *the drum*). *Hens Under the Hill* keeps its
   goblins and its tone. The Warren itself still goes to the Reach.
5. **The Fens step waits.** Phase 1 is the Vale only, and the Fens follow once you've played the new Vale and settled
   the 75-level question. The Fens' current four stay as they are meanwhile (an honest exception, noted in the GDD).
6. **No wall dressing in phase 1.** Each look comes from:
   - the floor and the scatter at the walls (niches become standing urn-and-skull shelves; banners become standards
     on poles);
   - the light's tint;
   - stalagmites and pools.

   Wall-face dressing is a later renderer change, measured on a phone.

## 6. As built: phase 1, with the Fens (2026-10-10)

The owner's call took the Fens in with the Vale, so phases 1 and 3 shipped together. Save v31.

- **The bands** (`src/sim/sites.js`, `band: true`; `roomLevelAt`): the Old Barrows 1–3, Wickham Keep 4–6, the Sunken
  Chapel 7–9, Toadking's Mound 10–12, the Canal Locks 13–15 and the Drowned Abbey 16–18. Each has three floors; the
  two rooms before a floor's hall are a level up, and the hall keeps its floor's level.
- **Floors with their own looks and foes** (`themes`, `families`): the Barrows' mouth is the Redhand's dig (the
  diggers), and its galleries are a crypt (`crypt`); the Keep's cellars go down into a cave (`cave`) the Redhand are
  digging; the Locks' third floor is Vat Seven, the old Sickpools, with the Harvest's people.
- **Out of the Vale:** the Tithe Mill and the Scrag Warren are parked for the Reach (`parked: true`), and the Mill
  stands on the overland as a landmark. The Sickpools' own site is gone. Saves made inside a gone site wake up at the
  dungeon that took it over (`GONE_SITES` in `persist/save.js`), and a save made inside a dungeon starts again at its
  first floor.
- **Bosses:** the Quartermaster (*Issue*) holds the Barrows' Muster Hall, and the Vatwarden (*Sluice*) holds Vat
  Seven. Both are new, and their looks are baked (variants B4 and FB5). The Standard holds the Chapel's last hall;
  Old Skarn is the Bailey's guest (the Keep, floor 1). The Fens' four keep their mechanics at their new halls.
  Measured in GDD §17 (all ten win at the hall's level, lowest HP 55–82 %).
- **Secret dungeons** (`secret: true`): the Ninth Milestone (9) and the Reedholm Undercroft (18). Each seals for two
  hours of play once its vault chest is opened (`SECRET_REST`, `state.secrets`), with a toast on the way out and at
  the sealed door.
- **Quests, the board and the compass:** every quest, trial, fragment and lead points at its new place (the Vale's
  errands run 1–9, Act II 10–18). Brannoc's *Standing Down* is in the Long Gallery, the Barrows' floor-2 hall,
  because the Quartermaster's own hall goes quiet once he falls. Each town's board sends you to the dungeon that holds
  your level (sometimes the one below), and a warden job only goes to a hall with no boss. A chest job's compass now
  makes for chests only (shrines have their own row), and goes down a floor when the floor runs out of them.
- **A repair to v1.47's reach** (GDD §17): the Choir's singers hold near her, and drummed shooters close to half their
  reach before they shoot.
