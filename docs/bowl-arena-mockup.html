The Bowl

The imperial Circus of the Sun in Solmere: half its tiers fallen in, Ma Gorrie taking the bets, the Crier still calling the bouts. Your company against another, in one room of sand. For now the rival companies are generated (authored names and manners, strength rolled from a seed), and the Bowl opens from ☰. The rules and the build are in bowl-arena-proposal.md.

The Sand

One room, from the game's camera (south-east, looking down). 44 × 34 tiles of raked sand, a large room. The near wall is knee-high so the floor reads; the far wall stands, with four tiers of crowd behind it. Your company comes in at the Dust Gate, theirs at the Sun Gate.

  1. 1The Sun's Box. Ma Gorrie with her slate; the Crier at the rail with his bell.
  2. 2The Sun Gate, theirs: they come in facing the camera, under their banner.
  3. 3The Dust Gate, yours: a low arch on the near side.
  4. 4The marks, two scuffed lines 16 tiles apart. Nothing else stands in the sand.
  5. 5Fallen tiers on the east: rubble and a broken arch, as canon has it.
  6. 6Eight braziers on the ring wall. Feast-day bouts are fought at dusk.

The bout's rules, in short

Levels evenedBoth companies fight at their bracket's top level. Gear stays as it is.
Two minutesA company with nobody standing loses. At 2:00 the Crier calls it on the share of HP standing.
One brainRivals run your companions' own AI, turned round, their priorities set by their manner.
Arena rulesCrowd control lasts half as long on a company's member; healing received × 0.75; no shrine boons carried in.
Safe sportDown in the Bowl is never Fallen: no temple, no Weakened, no cost.
Marks onlyFive paid bouts a day. No XP or gold, so it never competes with the dungeons.
