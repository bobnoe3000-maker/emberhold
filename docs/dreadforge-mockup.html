Content

Voxels & creatures

Props are voxel volumes baked to shaded, outlined sprites (4 facings free by rotation). Creatures are mirrored cellular-automata silhouettes — the two glowing eyes are the whole "alive, not rock" signal.

What this is. A from-spec implementation of the Dreadforge nightmare pipeline (the referenced dreadforge.html prototype wasn't in the handoff, so this is built from the TDD). Every canvas runs the real generators — value-noise fbm terrain, ramp-index tile shading, the voxel-to-sprite bake, CA creatures, and the glow/flicker/fog post stack — from a single integer seed. It targets Emberhold's locked iso grid, so the geometry is the same one already in the build; what changes is the material/palette system, the prop & creature generators, and the post stack.
