Top bar tidy: mockup

The owner (2026-10-05): "Lets clean up and align the hud a bit. The weather icons line can move left, making room to shift the mini map and buttons below up." Proposal: the top bar becomes two tidy lines. On line 1 the place name sits on the left, and gold and cinders sit on the right, ending exactly on the minimap's edge. Line 2 sits under the place name: the sky dial and weather first, then the wages, both still tappable. Nothing sits on line 2 over on the right, so the minimap goes up to just under line 1, and the compass and Journal follow it up. That gains 22 px for the minimap and both buttons, and every right edge lines up at 12 px. Both phones use real captures of the game: the Vale at night in rain, a party of three, 390×844, with no notch inset.

Proposed

  1. The ☰ button spans both lines (34 px = 16 + 3 + 16 − 1), so its top and bottom line up with the text.
  2. Line 1: the place name on the left. Gold and cinders are on the right, ending on the minimap's right edge (12 px from the screen edge).
  3. Line 2, under the place name: the sky dial, the weather and the time of day, then the wages, in one row with one left edge. It stops 118 px short of the right side, so it never runs under the minimap. Each item keeps its own tap pad (44 px tall), and the pads touch nothing else.
  4. The minimap's frame starts 6 px under line 1 (y 36, was 58). The compass (152, was 174) and the Journal (204, was 226) follow it up with the same 8 px gaps.
  5. Every right edge is 12 px in from the screen edge: the gold and cinders, the minimap, the compass, the Journal.
  6. A shrine's boons and Weakened get a third line of their own, only while lit (top right). They never share line 1.

Shrine boons and Weakened lit

A third line, only while something's lit. The bar's bottom grows with them, so map labels and the room pill move down clear of the chips. Tap a chip and it says which shrine it came from.

How it would be built

  • index.html: #hud becomes a 3-column grid (☰ · name and line 2 · gold and cinders), two 16 px rows.
  • src/ui/hud.js: the boon and Weakened chips move off line 1 into a line-3 row (#hudLine3, hidden when empty). Each is its own chip with its own tap, and their text gets shorter: "ATK +25% 1:52" instead of "might +25% ATK 1:52".
  • src/ui/hud.js: the sky dial and wages move into one line-2 row under the name (#hudLine2), each keeping its own button and tap pad. It publishes --hud-l1 (line 1's bottom) beside --hud-b.
  • src/render/renderer.js: mmTop() reads line 1's bottom + 6, not the bar's bottom + 10. MM_RIGHT becomes 12. The label rule that keeps plaques clear of the buttons takes the new tops.
  • src/ui/compass.js, journal.js: top becomes the minimap's bottom + 8 / + 60 (one --mm-b variable), so the buttons can't drift from the minimap again.
  • Phone on its side and desktop: the same grid. On a wide screen the EMBERFALL wordmark comes back after the ☰, as it does now.
