Luminati's worlds

On a phone: drag on the world to walk (to the edge to run), Talk to speak and turn a page, two fingers to zoom. With a keyboard: WASD or the arrows, Shift to run, Space to talk, Q / E to turn the camera, the wheel to zoom. In a fight, Auto lets your hero fight by itself; touch the world to take over.

A prototype. The worlds are Luminati's (gitlab.com/stubborn-hug/lumina, MIT: licence), their villagers drawn as Emberfall's people. Nothing here touches your game. Back to the game · The notes
